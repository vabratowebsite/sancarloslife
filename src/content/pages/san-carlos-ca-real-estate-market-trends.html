GUIDE TO INTERPRET DATA

Average Home Prices & Home Values

Median Price. The midpoint or average price of all properties sold.

Sale to List Price. The mean sale price is divided by the mean list price. A number over 100 means on average, homes sold during that month were bought at a price higher than the listing price. A number below 100 means the opposite.

Days On Site

This number shows how many days on average it takes for a property to get sold as soon as it goes for sale in the market. A relatively high number indicates that real estate sales are slower, while a low number indicates that sales are fast.

Median Days on Site. The midpoint number of days a property became active on the MLS.

MARKET DATA QUESTIONS

What does this market trends page show?

Recent San Carlos sales activity: median price, sale-to-list ratio, days on market, active and new listings, and a sample of recent sold homes. Think of it as a monthly snapshot for buyers and sellers who want to know which way the market is leaning without reading every headline about interest rates.

What does median sale price actually mean?

It's the middle number — half the homes that sold went for more, half for less. It's useful for tracking direction over time, but it doesn't tell you what any specific home is worth. In a small city like San Carlos, a few big or small sales can pull the median around month to month, so trends are more reliable than any single data point.

What does sale-to-list price tell me?

It compares what a home sold for against its asking price. Above 100% means homes are generally going for over asking; below 100% means they're closing under. It's one of the cleanest signals of how aggressive buyers are right now.

What do days on market tell buyers and sellers?

A low number suggests homes are moving fast and buyers are decisive. A higher number usually means buyers are taking their time, pricing is stretched, or both. It varies by price band — entry-level homes often move faster than the top of the market even in a slower season.

How should buyers use this data?

Use it to set expectations on competition, pricing, and pace. If days on market are short and sale-to-list is above 100%, plan for stronger offers and faster decisions. If the numbers have softened, you may have more room to negotiate on price, contingencies, or repairs.

How should sellers use this data?

It tells you what buyers are willing to pay right now and how long they're taking to commit. Pair it with a property-specific review — recent comps on your street and in your school zone matter more than citywide medians.

Why do San Carlos numbers move so much month to month?

The city is small. Maybe 20–30 single-family homes close in a typical month, and one $5 million sale or two distressed condos can shift the median noticeably. That's why we look at three- and six-month trends in addition to the latest month.

Are these numbers guaranteed?

No. Market data is a snapshot, sourced from MLS, and should be considered reliable but not guaranteed. Always verify current information and get a property-specific review before making financial decisions.

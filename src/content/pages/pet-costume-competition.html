PET COSTUME CONTEST

“Calling all pet lovers! Think your pet has the best pet costume?”

Winner will receive a $100 gift card of their choice.

Entry submission deadline is October 31.

RULES

1

Take A Photo of Your Pet: Snap a photo of yourself with your pet in costume anda solo shot of your pet in costume for the contest. Email both to petcostume@vabrato.com (Only the solo pic of your pet will make it onto our social media spotlight.)

2

Be Creative!: Dress up your pet however you like —go wild with your ideas! From spooky to silly, we want to see it all. Just keep it family-friendly, please.

3

Prize: The winner will receive a $100 gift card to Bow Wow Meow or any gift card of their choice!

4

U.S. Pets Only: Sorry, this contest is for U.S. pets only.

5

Costume Deadline: Make sure to submit your pet’s costume entry by October 31st.

6

Winner Revealed: We’ll announce the best-dressed pet on November 8. Stay tuned for the big reveal on social media and via email!

7

No Filter, Just Fur: Keep it natural! No heavy photo filters or editing, No AI—let your pet's adorable costume do all the talking!

8

One Pet, One Entry: Only one costume entry per pet, so choose your best look! But feel free to dress up the rest of the furry squad, just not in the contest.

9

All Pets Welcome: Dogs, cats, bunnies, birds, reptiles—if they can rock a costume, they can enter! All species are encouraged to join the fun!

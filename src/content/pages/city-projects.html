DOWNTOWN PROJECTS

Downtown

Downtown Specific Plan

The City's 20-year guide for land use, building design and mobility downtown. In effect since February 25, 2026. [2]

Now: Being carried out
Latest: June 22, 2026

Downtown

Downtown Streetscape Master Plan

The adopted design for downtown streets and plazas, built in phases as funding allows. [2, 3]

Now: Phase 1A building
Latest: June 22, 2026

Downtown

Harrington Park Refresh

The first construction project of the downtown plan: a plaza, restroom building, seating and rain garden. Completion expected early 2027. [3]

Now: Under construction
Latest: September 2026

Downtown

Centennial Plaza (700 block of Laurel)

The car-free 700 block of Laurel Street is designed to become a permanent plaza with a pavilion and stage. $10 million set aside; construction expected spring 2027 to spring 2029. [4, 5]

Now: Construction documents
Latest: August 24, 2026

The adopted plan for 145 acres north of Holly Street: up to 1,890 new homes plus life-science and other development. [6, 7]

Parks and recreation

Aquatic center at Laureola Park

Laureola Park is the preferred site for a long-requested public pool. About $12.8 million for the pool alone; no funding approved yet. [8]

Now: Site selected

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

San Carlos City projects: common questions

How do I comment at a San Carlos City Council meeting?

Fill out a Request to Speak form in the Council Chambers at City Hall, 600 Elm Street, or join on Zoom and use the raise-hand feature (press *9 by phone). Questions about participating go to cityclerk@cityofsancarlos.org. [12]

When does the San Carlos City Council meet?

Regular City Council meetings begin at 7:00 p.m. in the Council Chambers at City Hall, 600 Elm Street; the City's E-News announces each meeting and its agenda. [12] The City posts its list of regularly scheduled meetings on its Public Meetings page. [14]

What are the biggest City projects in San Carlos right now?

As of September 2026: the Measure G sales tax on the November 3 ballot, the Harrington Park Refresh downtown, the planned Centennial Plaza on the 700 block of Laurel Street, the Northeast Area Specific Plan, a proposed aquatic center at Laureola Park, the Parks Master Plan and US 101/Holly Street safety improvements. [1, 3, 2, 6, 8, 9, 11]

How does San Carlos Life report on City projects?

We read City agendas, staff reports, adopted resolutions and meeting videos, label each fact by the strength of its source, and link every fact to that source. We do not endorse or oppose City projects or ballot measures, and we correct errors publicly.

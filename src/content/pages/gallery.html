SEND IN YOUR PHOTOS

The San Carlos Photo Gallery is a collection of photos from everyone in San Carlos – meant to tell the story of our city through pictures. Please feel free to send in your photo using the form below.

Should your photos be chosen, they will be posted on the San Carlos Life website and social media with credit to yourself. The basic rules are (1) the photo was taken in San Carlos, and (2) the photo was taken by yourself.

At the end of the year, on December, the team of San Carlos Life will choose the best photo from all that was sent, and the photographer will win a gift certificate courtesy of Vabrato Real Estate.
